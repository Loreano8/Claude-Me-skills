# CVCasa (formerly CV Studio)

- What it is: a mobile-first website (cvcasa.com, works on iPhone in the browser) and an Android app that turns a job seeker's old CV, a few messy lines or their voice into a clean, up-to-date CV, adapts it to each job offer with a before→after match score, writes the cover letter, and helps with the rest of the application (interview sheet, application email, scanner and single-PDF file, follow-up reminders, daily job alerts).
- Who it is for: job seekers in French-speaking West and Central Africa (Benin first, then Togo, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Niger, Cameroon): young graduates, people changing jobs, public-exam ("concours") candidates and people applying abroad. Mostly on mobile phones, paying with Mobile Money. The site is also in English and shows prices in local currency for visitors elsewhere (paid in crypto outside the FCFA zone).
- What it sells, at what price: building and downloading a CV is free. Paid actions use credits, no subscription: 3 credits = 500 FCFA, 10 = 1,500 FCFA, 25 = 3,000 FCFA (launch offer until 31 Dec 2026: 5 / 16 / 35 credits for the same prices). 1 credit per AI action (generate or improve the CV, adapt to an offer, cover letter, interview sheet, translation); 2 credits for a permanent "Pro version" of one CV (no footer, all templates, Word). 1 free credit at sign-up.
- Where and how: online only, cvcasa.com and an Android APK distributed directly (not on the Play Store). Payment by Mobile Money through FedaPay (Benin, Togo, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Niger) or USDT/USDC on BEP20. Acquisition planned through WhatsApp, TikTok/Facebook short videos, SEO guide pages, a referral programme and an ambassador programme (commission paid by Mobile Money).
- Budget and constraints: solo non-technical founder in Benin working with an AI developer; hosting on free tiers; AI paid per use (about 7–18 FCFA per action); no advertising budget decided; 6 accounts, 0 paying customer besides the founder's own test purchases.

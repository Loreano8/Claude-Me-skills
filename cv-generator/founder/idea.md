# CV Studio

- What it is: a mobile-first website (works on iPhone through the browser) and an Android app that helps a job seeker build a professional, up-to-date CV in French or English with AI, adapt it to each job offer, write the cover letter and interview preparation, and assemble and keep the paper documents a job application needs (scanner, single-PDF application file, document safe).
- Who it is for: job seekers in French-speaking West and Central Africa (Benin first, then Togo, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon…): young graduates looking for a first job, workers changing jobs, candidates for public-service exams ("concours") and people applying abroad (Canada, France, Europe). Mostly on mobile phones, paying with Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Moov Money).
- What it sells, at what price:
  - Free: fill in the CV (by hand, by voice, or from photos of an old paper CV or handwritten notes), 3 basic templates, PDF export with a small "Created with CV Studio" mention, scanner up to 3 pages per PDF, application file (CV + letter + 2 documents in one PDF), document safe, application tracker.
  - Credits (no subscription): 3 credits = 500 FCFA, 10 = 1,500 FCFA, 25 = 3,000 FCFA (655.957 FCFA = 1 EUR). 1 free credit at sign-up (once per device and per internet connection).
    - 1 credit per AI action: generate the CV, correct it by voice, adapt it to a job offer (with before→after match score), cover letter, interview prep sheet, translation, analysis of a template the user likes, one handwritten page turned into typed text.
    - 2 credits: "Pro version" of one CV, permanent for that CV (no mention, all 13 templates, Word export of CV and letter).
    - 1 credit: a scanned PDF of more than 3 pages, or an application file with more than 2 extra documents.
  - Referral: when an invited friend makes a first purchase, both get 1 credit.
- Where and how: online only. Site https://cv-studio-site.onrender.com (short link bit.ly/cv_studio), Android APK distributed directly (Play Store not yet published). Payment by Mobile Money through FedaPay (live). Acquisition planned through WhatsApp statuses, TikTok/Facebook videos (4 short ads already produced), referral.
- Budget and constraints: solo non-technical founder in Benin with an AI developer; hosting 0 $/month (free tiers); AI paid per use (Anthropic, measured 7–18 FCFA per action on the current model); FedaPay fee ~1.8 %; no domain name yet (no email sending yet); advertising budget not decided; 1 real paying user (the founder) + 1 trusted tester using it for real.

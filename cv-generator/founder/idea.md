# CV Studio

- What it is: a website (works on iPhone through the browser) and an Android app that writes a professional, up-to-date CV in French or English with AI, adapts it to each job offer, and writes the cover letter and the interview preparation.
- Who it is for: job seekers in French-speaking West and Central Africa (Benin first, then Togo, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon…): young graduates looking for a first job, workers changing jobs, candidates for public-service exams ("concours") and people applying abroad (Canada, France, Europe). Mostly on mobile phones, paying with Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Moov Money).
- What it sells, at what price:
  - Free, no account: fill in the CV, 13 templates, PDF and Word export, photo and signature, automatic advice.
  - AI actions cost 1 credit each (generate the CV from messy text or voice, correct it by voice, adapt it to a job offer pasted as text/PDF/screenshot with a before→after match score, cover letter, interview prep sheet, translation to English for US/UK/Canada norms, analysis of a CV template the user likes). 1 free credit at sign-up (once per device and per internet connection).
  - Credit packs, no subscription: 3 credits = 500 FCFA, 10 credits = 1,500 FCFA, 25 credits = 3,000 FCFA (655.957 FCFA = 1 EUR).
  - Also: application tracker, testimonials ("I got the job") published only with consent and after manual review.
- Where and how: online only. Site https://cv-studio-site.onrender.com (short link bit.ly/cv_studio), Android APK (Play Store not yet published). Customers will find it through WhatsApp status, TikTok and Facebook videos/ads (planned). Payment by Mobile Money through FedaPay (live since 25 Oct 2026; card payment pending account validation).
- Budget and constraints:
  - Solo founder in Benin, non-technical; the product is already built and live.
  - Hosting costs 0 $/month (free tiers: Render static site, Supabase database + server function). AI = Anthropic API, paid per use from a prepaid balance (currently about 5 $).
  - FedaPay fee observed: 9 FCFA on a 500 FCFA payment.
  - Play Store: 25 $ developer account, Google requires its own billing (15 % fee) for digital goods sold inside the Play Store app.
  - Advertising budget: not decided yet.
  - Status: 1 real paying user so far (the founder's own test purchase). No marketing done yet.

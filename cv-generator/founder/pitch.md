CV Studio — a website and an Android app (link shared on WhatsApp/TikTok: bit.ly/cv_studio).
Paste a job offer you received (text, PDF or a screenshot): the AI adapts your CV to that offer in about 15 seconds and shows a match score before → after (example: 68 → 86). It also writes the cover letter and lists the interview questions to prepare.
You can also just talk: tell your career out loud and the AI writes a clean, professional CV. It never invents degrees or jobs.
Creating your CV and downloading it as PDF or Word is free, no account needed. The first AI action is free after signing up.
After that: 500 FCFA for 3 AI actions, 1,500 FCFA for 10, 3,000 FCFA for 25. Pay with MTN MoMo or Moov Money. No subscription, nothing is charged afterwards.

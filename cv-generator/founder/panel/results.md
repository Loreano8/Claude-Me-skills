## What would flip a no

- Seeing someone I know get an interview or a concours result because of it, or a lot more free actions so I can see results before paying anything.
- If I got an offer for a job I really want and the free action showed me it truly raised my chances, I might pay 500 FCFA once for that one offer.
- If my cousin in Canada told me the AI makes a proper Canadian-style CV and someone actually got a callback from it, I would pay 500 FCFA for the applications over there.
- If a friend or someone in my group tells me they got an interview because of the adapted CV, then I would pay 500 FCFA for a job I really want.
- If someone I know actually got called for an interview because of this, and told me so on WhatsApp.
- If the free AI action got me called for an interview, or a friend in my WhatsApp group showed me it worked for him, I would pay 500 FCFA before the next offer.
- If a friend showed me that the adapted CV actually got her called for an interview for a real offer.
- A real interview call for a job I applied to with the adapted CV. Then I might pay 500 FCFA once for the next offer.
- Someone I know personally telling me they got called for an interview after using it, and a free try that shows me the result before I pay anything.
- Someone I know, a friend or relative, telling me they paid, it worked, and they got called for an interview.
- Someone I know getting an interview or a job after using it, and having a real offer in hand that I really want to get.
- A friend I trust telling me they got an interview with a CV adapted by it, and a real job offer coming that I want to apply for.
